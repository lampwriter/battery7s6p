# 7S6P 18650 module: top and bottom bus boards (v1)

KiCad 9.0.2. The boards are generated by `gen.py` (pcbnew API). `build.sh` regenerates them, fills the zones, runs DRC and renders. `export.sh` writes the JLCPCB outputs to `fab/`.

## v1.1: 1 oz copper variant
`gen.py` reads `CU_OZ` (default 2). `CU_OZ=1 ./build_1oz.sh` builds the 1 oz boards into `top_1oz/` and `bot_1oz/`; `./export_1oz.sh` writes `fab_1oz/`. Order those with **1 oz outer copper** (2 layers, 1.6 mm, ENIG): `fab_1oz/battery7s6p_top_1oz_gerbers.zip` and `fab_1oz/battery7s6p_bot_1oz_gerbers.zip`.

On the 1 oz top board the XT90 moves 23 mm down (y=62 to y=85) so the N7 tail spans rows 0–3 and the neck can carry the current. Cell fuses are 2.0 mm wide on 1 oz (v1 is 1.0 mm on 2 oz), the same copper cross-section and the same blow current. Balance-tap fuses widen from 0.25 mm to 0.45 mm. Every high-current path stays at about 9 C rise or under at 30 A (`fuse_calc_1oz.txt`; the XT90 pad wrap is the warmest, about 9.4 C). The v1 2 oz boards and `fab/` outputs are unchanged.

## Arrangement
- 7 columns (series groups G1 to G7) by 6 rows (6 parallel cells per group). Pitch is 20.25 mm and the cell array is 141.75 x 121.5 mm. Each board is 182.75 x 134.5 mm, which includes a 35 mm connector tab.
- Cell orientation alternates by column. Odd groups have + up and even groups have - up.
- Nodes N0 (pack -) to N7 (pack +):
  - The top board busses N1 (G1+/G2-), N3 (G3+/G4-), N5 (G5+/G6-) and N7 (G7+, pack +).
  - The bottom board busses N0 (G1-, pack -), N2, N4 and N6.
- Each board's cell side is its **B (bottom) layer**, and the connectors sit on the outer F side. The bottom board is designed as seen from below, so it's mirrored and its tab ends up under the G1 end.
- Because 7S is odd, pack - (N0) is on the bottom board and pack + is on the top board. N0 reaches the top board's XT90 through one **10 AWG jumper with crimped M5 ring lugs** (about 250 mm) between LUG1 on each board. Bolted, no solder.
- Bottom taps N0, N2, N4 and N6 reach the top board through a **4-pin JST-XH female-female straight cable** (bottom J1 to top J3).

## Holder
This design uses the common square interlocking 18650 bracket: 20.25 mm centre pitch, 18.3/18.5 mm bore, 21.7 mm single cell, about 9 mm thick, ABS+PC V0. It's sold as 1P/2P/3P snap-together pieces (source: chinabatterymachine.com/tmaxcn.com spec table). Buy the **20.25 mm pitch** version, not the 19.0, 19.2 or 19.5 mm variants.

## Mounting
- Ten M3 holes (3.2 mm) at interstitial points between cells, mirror-symmetric so the top and bottom boards line up. Clamp the stack with M3 threaded rod through the holder's corner gaps.
- Two more M3 holes in the tab.
- Check the actual holder before ordering. If its corner gaps can't pass M3 rod, move the holes in `gen.py`.

## Cell contacts (no welding, no solder on cells)
- Positive end: a 6 mm ENIG pad on the cell side, with 6 vias in the pad to the outer layer. Only that pad is positive copper on the cell side. Nothing else on the cell side is copper within the 18.5 mm cell circle, so the bare can shoulder can't short.
- Negative end: a 12 mm ENIG pad, solid into a cell-side pour. That pour exists only under negative-end columns, where the cans are the same net.
- Solder mask covers everything except the contact pads.
- **Fit a fish-paper insulating ring on every + end.** It's marked on the silkscreen.
- Order with **ENIG**, and with **via-in-pad (epoxy filled and capped)** if you want flat + pads.
- Contact pressure comes from clamping the two boards against the holder stack.
- A fully placed SMD contact from JLCPCB/LCSC wasn't found in time. The candidates are MYOUNG MY-18650-04-J (+, C19184125) and MY-18650-05-J (-, C19184124), but their current rating, footprint and SMT suitability are unverified. They're not in the BOM.

## Fusing (one fuse per cell, at its + end)
- Each + island connects to the outer bus pour through a **1.0 mm x about 3.5 mm** 2 oz trace, inside a 6.5 mm no-pour circle.
- Numbers from `fuse_calc.py` / `fuse_calc.txt`:
  - IPC-2221 (external layer, 2 oz): 5.4 A at 20 C rise and 7.3 A at 40 C rise. Normal duty is 30 A / 6 = 5 A per cell.
  - Onderdonk (adiabatic melt): about 20 A in 1 s, 64 A in 100 ms and 200 A in 10 ms.
  - An internally shorted cell is fed by its 5 neighbours (well over 100 A), so the link should open in milliseconds.
- Onderdonk ignores heat sinking. The short link and the mask make the real blow current higher, so bench-test one board with a current source before trusting it.
- Negative ends are not fused (one fuse per cell is enough). Change `FUSE_W` in `gen.py` to retune.

## Bus (30 A)
- 2 oz copper.
- Outer-layer pours span the whole node width (40 mm or more), and the cell-side pours are added under the negative columns.
- Stitching vias (0.5/0.9 mm) sit only in negative-end columns, so there's no positive copper on the cell side near the cans.
- The tab pours are on both layers and carry the XT90 current.

## Connectors (JLCPCB THT assembly, see `fab/*BOM*`, `fab/*CPL*`)
| Ref | Part | LCSC |
|---|---|---|
| Top J1 | AMASS XT90PW-M PCB male, right angle (pad 1 = PACK+ N7, pad 2 = PACK- N0) | C3040689 |
| Top J2 | JST B8B-XH-A balance: pin 1 = N0 (0 V), pin 2 = N1 ... pin 8 = N7 (7S+) | C157972 |
| Top J3 / Bottom J1 | JST B4B-XH-A, inter-board tap link N0/N2/N4/N6 | C144395 |
- Every balance tap has a 0.25 mm x 3 mm fuse link. That's about 2 A continuous, and it melts at about 16 A in 100 ms.
- On the top board, J2/J3 pin 1 is the separate net `N0_SENSE`. It's connected to N0 only through the bottom board and cable, which is intentional.

## Fab settings
- 2 layers, 1.6 mm, **2 oz outer copper**, ENIG, any mask colour.
- Upload `battery7s6p_top_gerbers.zip` and `battery7s6p_bot_gerbers.zip` as separate orders.
- The CPL coordinates are footprint origins (pin 1), not centroids. Check the part placement and rotation in JLCPCB's assembly preview.

## DRC
Rules: 0.2 mm track and clearance, 0.3 mm hole, 0.6 mm via, 0.4 mm copper-to-edge. Zone clearance is 0.5 mm. Results:
- 0 errors and 0 unconnected items.
- Remaining warnings are harmless:
  - "footprint library battery7s6p not configured": the cell contacts are script-generated footprints.
  - XT90 silk overhangs the edge, because the connector intentionally overhangs by 2.6 mm.

## Open items
- Check XT90 polarity against the housing. The XT90-S anti-spark version is a cable connector, so put the XT90-S on the mating harness side. The PCB part is the standard XT90PW.
- Revolectrix balance pin order is assumed to be standard JST-XH (pin 1 = pack -). Check it against your adapter board.
- There's no BMS or balancer in v1. Real-world fuse-blow testing is still to do.
