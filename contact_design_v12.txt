battery7s6p v1.2 - PRESSURE-CONTACT DESIGN NOTE (2026-11-01)
===============================================================
Variant: 1 oz Cu, ENIG, 2 layers, **0.8 mm FR4** (order option: thickness 0.8 mm). Build: kicad10-env bash build_v12.sh && kicad10-env bash export_v12.sh
v1 (2 oz) and v1.1 (1 oz) are unchanged and still reproducible (gen.py with CU_OZ=2/1; V12=1 selects v1.2).

1. WHAT WAS CHOSEN
------------------
Solder-free, weld-free compression stack per module end:
  rigid backing plate | 3.2 mm silicone foam pad | PCB v1.2 (0.8 mm, flexure islands) | cells (proud of HDPE) ... same mirrored at other end
  12 x M4 rods inside 1/4in OD spacer tubes set a HARD STOP, so the foam squeeze (= contact force) is fixed by geometry, not by
  how hard the nuts are torqued.

What the PCB does (manufactured feature, no parts): every cell pad sits on its own ~18 x 18 mm "island". Islands are separated by
2 mm routed slots on every interior grid line and joined only by 10 mm bridges at the middle of each cell edge. At each of the 30
interstitial points the slots meet in a 6.5 mm hole (rod/tube passes here; 12 used, the rest are spare/flex). The thin 0.8 mm board
plus slots lets each cell's island tilt/deflect into the foam independently, so cell length variation is taken up by the foam
behind that cell instead of the whole stiff board riding on the longest cells. Copper is pulled back 1.25 mm from all rod holes.

Why not spring contacts (researched):
  - LCSC/JLCPCB-placeable spring contacts are all low-force: Harwin S7041-42R (LCSC C21394881, 5 A, 1.2 N, 4.0 mm free / 2.8 mm min,
    ~1189 in stock, ~$0.34-0.45), Harwin S1741-46R (C22427402, 6 A, 0.49 N, 295 stock). Harwin's 7-14 A multi-directional parts
    (S1951-46R 7 A, S1941-42R 9 A, S1991/S1961-46R 14 A; travel only 0.4-0.7 mm) were not found on LCSC.
    Spec contact resistance <=20 mOhm at 1-2 N -> up to 0.5 W per contact at 5 A; 10 boards x 42 ends x 2-3 contacts = 840-1260 parts.
  - LCSC pogo pins: 1-2.5 A, 50-100 mOhm. MYOUNG 18650 holder springs (e.g. MY-18650-05-J C19184124) have no current rating and
    a ~20 mOhm wire-coil path (flashlight users bypass them for this reason).
  -> No acceptable JLCPCB-placeable 5 A cell contact exists. High-force flat pressure (~25 N) on ENIG gives ~1-3 mOhm instead.
Sources: harwin.com/products/S1951-46R, Harwin C055 spec PDF, lcsc.com/product-detail/C21394881.html, lcsc.com/category/742.html,
Rogers BISCO HT-800 datasheet (rogerscorp.com).

2. CALCULATIONS
---------------
CURRENT CHECK v1.2 (1oz, IPC-2221 external, target dT<=20C)
  Column-to-column bridge, typical (one cell's 5 A), 10 mm F only: dT   1.2 C
  Column-to-column bridge, 2x unequal sharing (10 A), 10 mm: dT   5.8 C
  Column-to-column bridge, 3x (15 A), 10 mm: dT  14.6 C
  All 6 bridges of one column line together, 30 A over 70 mm: dT   2.9 C
  N7 (top)/N0 (bot) end column, rows4+5 -> tail via row-line-4 bridge, 10 A over 15 mm: dT   3.0 C
  Row-line bridge inside a column (cell-to-cell sharing), 5 A over 10 mm: dT   1.2 C
  Unchanged from v1.1 (copper not touched): N7 tail 4.3 C, XT90 pad 9.4 C, N0 zones 1.4-1.7 C, M5 lug 3.2 C, cell fuses identical.

FLEXURE / FORCE
  Bridge (10 mm wide, 0.8 mm FR4, eff. hinge length 4.0 mm): EI=8533 N*mm^2, rot. stiffness 2133 N*mm/rad
   neighbour cell 0.1 mm shorter -> up to ~4.2 N lost to 4 bridges
   neighbour cell 0.2 mm shorter -> up to ~8.4 N lost to 4 bridges
   neighbour cell 0.3 mm shorter -> up to ~12.6 N lost to 4 bridges
  (same bridge in 1.6 mm FR4 would be 8x stiffer -> why v1.2 is 0.8 mm)
  Foam: BISCO HT-800 3.18 mm (1/8in), CFD 67 kPa typ (41-97 spec) @25%; area/cell 377 mm^2
   -> nominal contact force 25.3 N/cell at 0.80 mm squeeze (spec range 15-37 N); ~32 N/mm
   cell -0.3 mm vs nominal: ~16 N (foam squeeze 16%)
   cell -0.1 mm vs nominal: ~22 N (foam squeeze 22%)
   cell +0.1 mm vs nominal: ~28 N (foam squeeze 28%)
   cell +0.3 mm vs nominal: ~35 N (foam squeeze 34%)
  Clamp load per end plate: 42 x 25.3 = 1061 N (238 lbf); 12 rods -> 88 N/rod (M4 A2-70 proof ~6.2 kN: >60x margin)
  6.35 mm 6061 plate, 40.5 mm rod grid: pressure 62 kPa, deflection ~0.42 um (negligible); 12 mm Baltic-birch ply also fine (~0.53 um)
  Contact loss @30 A (6 x 5 A/cell), assume 2 mOhm per ENIG-to-Ni contact at ~25 N: 84 contacts x 25 A^2 x R = 4.2 W
  Stack: plate | foam 3.18->2.39 | PCB 0.8 | cell 65.1 (measure yours) | PCB 0.8 | foam | plate -> tube length 71.47 mm(Length variation is shared by the foam at both ends of a cell, so the numbers above are conservative.)
Recommended: sort cells by length (calipers) so each module is within +/-0.1 mm -> every cell 22-28 N.

3. HARDWARE (per module = 1 top + 1 bottom board)
-------------------------------------------------
  2x backing plate, 182.75 x 134.5 mm (or array area + 10 mm), 1/4in 6061 aluminum or 12 mm Baltic birch plywood; drill per
     battery7s6p_v12_drill_template.dxf layer ROD_HOLES_6p5 (use 4.5 mm in the plates if you prefer rods without tubes there);
     cut a window for XT90/J2/J3 (they stand up on the F side, the side facing the plate).
  2x foam pad: Rogers BISCO HT-800 (medium) silicone foam 1/8in (3.18 mm), or a generic "medium firm closed-cell silicone sponge
     1/8in"; cut to array size with 6.5 mm rod holes. NOT polyurethane/EPDM foam (creeps/compression-sets under battery heat).
  12x M4 threaded rod (A2 stainless or 8.8 zinc), ~100 mm, + 24 nuts + 24 washers (nylock or lock washers on one end).
  12x spacer tube 1/4in OD (6.35), ID >= 4.3 mm (e.g. aluminum or brass tube), cut to inner plate-to-plate gap
     = measured cell length + 1.6 + 4.77 mm (71.5 mm for 65.1 mm cells). All 12 tubes identical length (+/-0.05 mm: face them).
  HDPE holders (your CNC): 7x6 cells at 20.25 mm pitch, 18.6 mm cell holes (layer CELL_HOLES_18p6), 6.5 mm holes at the 12 rod
     points (min web ~1.8 mm, same as cell-to-cell web). Holder thickness must leave cells proud by >= 1.5 mm each end.
  2x M3 x (foam thickness ~2.4 mm) nylon spacers + screws at HT holes near the connectors to tie that corner to the plate.
  Insulation: Kapton (polyimide) adhesive ring 0.05-0.1 mm (or fish paper <= 0.15 mm) on every + shoulder. The ring must be thinner
     than your cells' + button protrusion (measure it); otherwise the ring, not the pad, carries the clamp force.
  Template: battery7s6p_v12_drill_template.dxf/.svg, mm, origin = PCB lower-left; pattern symmetric, same for both ends.

4. ASSEMBLY
-----------
  1. Solder XT90, J2, J3 (top) / J1 (bot) and the M5 lug jumper as before.
  2. Sort cells by length; put insulating rings on all + ends; load cells into the two HDPE holders with polarity per silkscreen.
  3. Stack: plate, foam, bottom PCB (B side/pads up), cell block, top PCB (pads down), foam, plate. Insert rods+tubes.
  4. Tighten nuts evenly in a cross pattern until every tube is snug (hard stop). Do not over-torque.
  5. Check: each cell group voltage on the balance lead; then at ~5-10 A load measure mV drop pad-to-cell on a few cells
     (target < 15 mV at 5 A per contact). Re-check after first heat cycles; foam keeps the force, tubes keep the geometry.

5. RISKS / TRADE-OFFS
---------------------
  - Contact resistance of a pressure joint (~1-3 mOhm) is higher than spot welds; ~4 W total at 30 A. Keep contacts clean (IPA).
  - Vibration on an ebike: foam keeps preload, but check periodically; consider a dab of contact grease (e.g. NO-OX-ID/Deoxit) on pads.
  - Bridges are thin FR4 hinges: handle bare boards carefully; don't flex panels by hand.
  - 0.8 mm board near XT90: support that corner (M3 standoffs); don't use the connector as a handle.
  - Flexure stiffness is an engineering estimate, not FEA: build one module and test with a few short/long cells first.
  - JLCPCB will route the slots (non-plated); no extra parts, BOM/CPL unchanged (connectors only, hand-soldered).
